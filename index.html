Tejal Mehta Lab

Modified and site‑specific drug delivery systems

The group designs micro- and nanocarrier formulations for the oral cavity, the eye, the skin and the vaginal route, and improves the dissolution of poorly soluble drugs.

Prof. Tejal MehtaProfessor and Head, Department of Pharmaceutics

Where a dose goes

Each route asks something different of a formulation: stay put in the mouth, get past the surface of the eye, hold a drug in the skin, cling to mucosa, or wait until the colon. Five stops, with the group's papers for each.

  1. 1 of 5

    Mouth

    Erlotinib liposomal gel for oral squamous cell carcinoma; electrospun nanofibres for locoregional therapy.

    55.27%tumour volume reduction in vivo with chitosan-coated erlotinib liposomal gel, against 38.88% for plain erlotinib gel.

    • Localized delivery of erlotinib using liposomal gel formulations for the treatment of oral squamous cell carcinoma2023, Int J Pharm

Funded project, ongoing

Formulation and development of nanofibers using electrospinning technique for locoregional therapy for oral cancer

Treating early oral cancer at the site of the tumour, so that less drug reaches healthy tissue. The group's earlier work loaded erlotinib into liposomes and a gel for local application in the mouth; the current project builds electrospun nanofibre systems for the same purpose.
